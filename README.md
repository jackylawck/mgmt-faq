## 繁體中文說明 (Traditional Chinese)

### 專案簡介
本知識庫專為高階經理人、跨功能專案負責人及 HRBP（人力資源業務合作夥伴）設計，旨在串聯經典管理學理論與企業治理實戰痛點。全站內容深度對齊 **英國特許管理學會 (CMI)** 與 **美國專業經理人學會 (ICPM)** 國際認證標準，提供繁體中文與英文雙語版本，並針對 MkDocs / GitHub Pages 進行最佳化排版。

* **模組 01：角色轉型與授權 (Role Transition & Delegation)**
* Katz 三大管理技能模型 (Technical, Human, Conceptual Skills)
* Mintzberg 10 大管理者角色 (Interpersonal, Informational, Decisional)
* 授權成熟度矩陣與主管上任前 90 天盤點清單


* **模組 02：策略解構與目標對齊 (Strategy Deconstruction & Alignment)**
* Mintzberg 意圖策略 vs. 湧現策略 (Deliberate vs. Emergent Strategy)
* Drucker MBO 目標管理與 SMART 原則邊界
* BSC 平衡計分卡與 OKR 敏捷目標的雙軌整合架構


* **模組 03：變革管理與組織韌性 (Change Management & Organizational Resilience)**
* Lewin 力場分析與三階段解凍模型 (Unfreeze, Change, Refreeze)
* Kotter 企業級八大變革步驟 (8-Step Change Model)
* Tuckman 跨部門團隊發展四階段 (Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing)


* **模組 04：決策思維與風險治理 (Decision Making & Risk Governance)**
* Herbert Simon 有限理性與滿意決策 (Bounded Rationality & Satisficing)
* Daniel Kahneman 快思慢想與董事會認知偏誤 (System 1 vs. System 2)
* James Reason 瑞士乾酪模型與縱深防禦 (Swiss Cheese Model & Defense in Depth)


* **模組 05：領導力與組織激勵 (Leadership & Motivation)**
* Herzberg 雙因素理論與工作豐富化 (Hygiene vs. Motivators)
* Victor Vroom VIE 期望理論與激勵鏈條診斷 (Expectancy, Instrumentality, Valence)
* Hersey-Blanchard 情境領導模型 (Directing, Coaching, Supporting, Delegating)

## English Description

### Overview

This repository serves as an executive knowledge base tailored for cross-functional managers, project leaders, and HRBPs. It bridges foundational management theories with real-world governance dilemmas. All frameworks are aligned with the international certification standards of the **Chartered Management Institute (CMI)** and the **Institute of Certified Professional Managers (ICPM)**, featuring full bilingual support (Traditional Chinese & English).

### Five Core Modules

* **Module 01: Role Transition & Delegation**
* Katz Three-Skill Model (Technical, Human, Conceptual Skills)
* Mintzberg's 10 Managerial Roles (Interpersonal, Informational, Decisional)
* Delegation Matrix & The First 90-Day Leadership Audit


* **Module 02: Strategy Deconstruction & Goal Alignment**
* Mintzberg's Deliberate vs. Emergent Strategy
* Drucker's MBO & SMART Goal Boundaries
* Dual Integration: Balanced Scorecard (BSC) & Objectives and Key Results (OKRs)


* **Module 03: Change Management & Organizational Resilience**
* Lewin's Force Field Analysis & Three-Stage Model (Unfreeze, Change, Refreeze)
* Kotter's 8-Step Change Process for Enterprise Transformation
* Tuckman's Stages of Team Development (Forming, Storming, Norming, Performing)


* **Module 04: Decision Making & Risk Governance**
* Herbert Simon's Bounded Rationality & Satisficing
* Daniel Kahneman's System 1 & System 2 Cognition and Bias Mitigation
* James Reason's Swiss Cheese Model & Defense in Depth


* **Module 05: Leadership & Motivation**
* Herzberg's Dual-Factor Theory & Job Enrichment
* Victor Vroom's VIE Expectancy Theory & Motivational Links
* Hersey-Blanchard Situational Leadership Model (SLII)
